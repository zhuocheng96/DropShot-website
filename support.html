常见问题 FAQ

需要注册账号吗？ Do I need an account?
不需要，打开就能用。No. Just open the app and start.

数据存在哪里？ Where is my data stored?
只存在你的 iPhone 上。删除 App 会删除数据，请定期导出备份。Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your data, so export a backup regularly.

怎么备份和换手机？ How do I back up or move to a new phone?
“我的 → 设置 → 导出备份”，保存到“文件”或 iCloud Drive；新手机上用“恢复备份”选择该文件。Me → Settings → Export Backup, save it to Files or iCloud Drive, then use Restore Backup on the new phone.

怎么切换语言？ How do I change the language?
“我的 → 设置 → 语言”。Me → Settings → Language.
